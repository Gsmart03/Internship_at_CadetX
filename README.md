# Internship_at_CadetX
**This project focuses on analysing heavy supplier, product, inventory, and warehouse data to understand and optimise supply-chain operations, inventory health, and warehouse efficiency. Warehouses handling heavy goods face complex challenges — stock imbalance, overstocking, stockouts, inefficient space use, unreliable suppliers, and inaccurate demand forecasting. Raw operational data exists, but without structured analysis it's hard to turn it into decisions. Your goal is to build a data-driven warehouse intelligence capability that helps an organisation:

Understand product movement and demand patterns
Measure inventory health and utilisation
Evaluate warehouse operational efficiency
Reduce stock risks and operational waste
Support better procurement and planning decisions**
